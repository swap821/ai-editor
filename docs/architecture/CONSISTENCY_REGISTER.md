# Consistency register

The operator, 2026-11-04: "I want u to make my cross-system consistency of my
project 10/10 honestly 100%."

## What this is

Many facts in GAGOS are relied on by more than one part of it: which brains
exist and which of them are cloud, what a task's statuses are, which port the
counter answers on, whether a change's tests passed. When two parts derive
such a fact separately they drift, and the drift is invisible until something
breaks. On 2026-11-03 alone four defects had that shape:

- the failover's list of cloud providers lacked `anthropic` for weeks;
- the Codex sealing flags existed twice;
- the counter could not reach the brains the workshop used;
- the privacy filter and the sandbox's report disagreed about a test's name.

This register lists those facts, every place each is derived, and its state:

| State | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `single` | One source; the other places import it, and a guard test fails when a second derivation appears. |
| `agreed` | Derived in several places on purpose (a frontend and a backend, say); a guard test fails when they disagree. |
| `open` | Neither, yet. It counts against the score. |

## How it is kept honest

- `.aios/state/CONSISTENCY_REGISTER.json` is the authority. The status region
  below is generated from it by `python scripts/consistency_audit.py --write`;
  history above it is prose.
- The audit checks every claim: each place's file exists and still names its
  symbol, and each guard is a real test. A row that claims a guard which is
  not there is an error, never a point. `tests/test_consistency_register.py`
  runs the audit on every backend run.
- The score is the share of registered facts that are `single` or `agreed`.
  100% means every registered fact is guarded. A fact nobody registered is not
  measured; when one is found, it is added, open, and the score drops until
  it is guarded.
- The audit also counts hand-written lists of provider names in `aios/` and
  `tools/`, a second copy of "which brains exist" being the shape most of
  2026-11-03's drift had.

## Adding a fact

Add a row with every place the fact is derived (`path` or `path::symbol`), its
state, and its guards (`tests/path.py::test_name`), then run
`python scripts/consistency_audit.py --write`. A row may be `open`; it may not
claim a guard it does not have.
